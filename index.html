📖 ComplianceShield User Guide & Walkthrough

          

          
        

        

          

            💡 Zero Setup Required: This application runs 100% in your browser. All assessment progress and code scan results are stored safely on your device without transmitting data over the internet.
          


          

            

              
1. Set Scope & Quick Audit

              
Enter your app name and domain. Click "Quick Audit" to auto-evaluate baseline TLS, Git branch protection, and backup configurations.

            


            

              
2. Test Industry Presets

              
Use the App Template dropdown in the top bar to load realistic profiles: SaaS (SOC 2), Telehealth (HIPAA), or FinTech (PCI-DSS).

            


            

              
3. Code & Secret Scanner

              
Switch to the "Code & Secret Scanner" tab. Test presets like SQL Injection or Hardcoded Secrets to see live line-by-line flaw detection and fixes.

            


            

              
4. Audit Checklist & Proof

              
Mark controls as ✓ Compliant or ✕ Gap. Click "View Audit Evidence Proof" to see exact screenshots & logs required by certified auditors.

            


            

              
5. Remediation Roadmap

              
Check the Remediation Roadmap tab for a 3-phase execution checklist (Weeks 1 to 6) to eliminate critical gaps prior to audit observation windows.

            


            

              
6. Policy Gen & PDF Export

              
Copy markdown policies (Incident Response, BCDR, HIPAA BAA). Click "Export Report" to print a formal PDF summary for leadership.
